What makes a Top Talent at Ecommpay?

A Top Talent consistently delivers exceptional results, keeps developing their expertise and helps others succeed. Their contribution is visible in business outcomes and in the way they work with colleagues.

Delivers exceptional results

Consistently exceeds agreed performance expectations and takes responsibility for outcomes.

Keeps growing

Builds professional expertise and strengthens the skills needed to work effectively with others.

Makes a business impact

Turns expertise into improvements that matter to the business, such as better client experiences, stronger results or more efficient processes.

Brings others forward

Shares knowledge, collaborates across teams and demonstrates our VICI values in everyday decisions.

How do you become a Top Talent?

Top Talent recognition is based on sustained performance, colleague feedback and executive calibration.

01 · PERFORMANCE
Above 120%

In each of two consecutive performance cycles — one year in total.

02 · 360° FEEDBACK
7+ average score

For soft skills, based on a minimum of five reviews.

03 · EXECUTIVE CALIBRATION
Validated together

The executive team reviews the evidence and validates Top Talent recognition during talent calibration.

Meeting the performance and feedback criteria is the basis for consideration. Final recognition is validated through executive calibration.

A package built around you.

Recognition should come with something tangible. The Top Talents package gives you time, personal development support and an additional financial reward.

+3 days
More time for yourself

Three additional loyalty days added to your annual leave allowance. Take them as time off during their validity period; unused days expire after one year.

$2,000
Development focused on your growth

A dedicated budget for coaching and learning for each Top Talent. It covers the talent year, from September to September, until the next talent calibration, and is funded by the company’s HR budget.

10%
A bonus that keeps its value

The company index is not applied to the Top Talent bonus: it remains 10% of your monthly salary. The payment will be made with your November salary.

Your questions answered

The payment will be made with your November salary. The bonus is 10% of your monthly salary, and the company index is not applied.

The $2,000 budget covers the talent year: from September to September, until the next talent calibration. It is funded by the company’s HR budget.

L&D processes your Jira request after your manager approves it. Before submitting a request for external learning in Jira, discuss your development with your manager in a 1:1 and agree your development priorities together.

For example, consider:

  • •Which skills do I need to maintain or strengthen to keep achieving my current goals?
  • •Which new skills will help me work more effectively?
  • •Which skills will help me take a process or project to the next level?
  • •Which knowledge will help me take on new tasks or greater responsibility?

Record these agreements in your individual development plan (IDP), making clear which skill you are developing, why you need it and what result you expect.

Development should make sense for both you and the company. When choosing learning, explain how the knowledge will help you achieve your goals, increase your impact and benefit your team and the business.

Once your development priority is agreed and recorded in your IDP, you can submit your request for external learning in Jira.
